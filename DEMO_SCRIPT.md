# Demonstration script

1. Unlock the phone and, if SmartWallet asks, confirm the fingerprint or pattern. Say that this lock is local and the Supabase session stays separate.
2. Show Home. Read the month label and the expense total. Point out that transfers are excluded.
3. Open a wallet and the activity list. Show that a transfer is visible there but is not added to the Home expense.
4. Open Plan, then Budgets, Goals, and Investments. State that goal contributions and investment prices are simulated or manual, not live trades.
5. Open Financial Studio. Read the four service lines:
   - LightGBM and XGBoost can be experimental.
   - Isolation Forest and SARIMAX stay on the statistical baseline when history is short.
   - `real_world_validated` is false.
6. Scroll to one on-device card and say it is calculated on the phone from the account records.
7. Open the assistant. Ask "combient j ai depense pour le food ce moi", then "et le mois dernier" and "pourquoi". Ask "comment economiser 300 tnd" and say the plan is a simulation. Ask "combien reste dans mon wallet" and check that the net balance matches Home. Mention that no remote language model is configured.
8. Open the scanner only if there is time. Show the confirmation screen and do not save unless you intend to add a real expense.
9. Close with Settings: language, theme, and the biometric switch.

Do not present the synthetic MAE or classification scores as the accuracy of this account.
